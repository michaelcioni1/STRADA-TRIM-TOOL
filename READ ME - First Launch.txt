STRADA Trim Tool — installing

1. Drag "STRADA Trim Tool" onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open STRADA Trim Tool from your Applications folder.

If macOS says the app "can't be opened" or "Apple could not verify" it:
This happens because the app was built privately rather than distributed
through Apple. It only needs to be approved once:

  1. Click "Done" (or "OK") on the warning.
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to the Security section. You'll see a note about STRADA Trim Tool.
     Click "Open Anyway", then confirm with your password or Touch ID.

After that, STRADA Trim Tool opens normally with a double-click.

The first time STRADA Trim Tool reads an external drive, macOS may ask for
permission to access removable volumes — click Allow.
